01 — Domain

Domain Section Summary

Literature-backed framing of problem context, research gap, objectives, methodology, and technologies used.

◆

Literature Survey

Sri Lankan farmers make important decisions on crops, fertiliser, market timing, and field resources under time-sensitive and uncertain conditions. Existing digital agriculture solutions are useful but mostly fragmented, with each system focusing on only one function. Research shows that better outcomes come from integrated platforms that combine AI, practical workflows, and real field data. IoT-enabled sensing is especially important for improving crop recommendation accuracy by using live soil and environmental conditions.

◎

Research Gap

Current solutions have major gaps: they are disconnected, difficult to use as one workflow, and often weak in local context adaptation. Many crop recommendation tools still depend mainly on manual input and do not fully use IoT-based field data. Farmers therefore spend extra effort combining information from multiple sources. An integrated AI + IoT platform is needed to bridge this gap.

?

Research Problem

Farmers lack a single reliable system to support all major decisions from planning to execution. Crop decisions are often made without continuous field sensing, which reduces recommendation reliability. The core problem is how to design one practical agriculture platform that integrates key functions and improves crop recommendation quality through IoT-supported real-time data.

✓

Methodology

The project used an iterative development approach with requirement analysis, architecture design, module implementation, and multi-level testing. A layered system was built with mobile frontend, backend APIs, cloud data services, and AI components. The crop module was designed with IoT-aware input context to improve prediction relevance. Final validation included API, integration, and functional workflow testing to ensure real-world usability.

02 — Research Objectives

Research Objectives

Integrated goals to build and validate practical decision support for Sri Lankan farmers.

01

Build and validate an integrated smart agriculture platform for Sri Lankan farmers.

02

Support fertiliser planning, IoT-based crop recommendation, market forecasting, and equipment/labour hiring in one workflow.

03

Improve usability and reliability for practical farmer decision-making contexts.

04

Strengthen practical decision support through testing and system integration.
